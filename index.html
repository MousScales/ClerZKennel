Who We Are

A Loving Home,
Not a Kennel

ClerZ Kennel is a family-operated breeder on Connecticut's East Shoreline, specializing in healthy, well-socialized Toy Poodle and Toy Poodle/Shih Tzu mix puppies. Every puppy is raised inside our home alongside both parents, receiving daily attention, early socialization, and a loving environment from birth.

We focus on producing small, hypoallergenic companion dogs with hair rather than fur — a great choice for families looking for low-shedding pets. Our puppies begin basic house training using pee pads, are accustomed to everyday household life, and are cared for as true members of the family.

  • Veterinary wellness examination
