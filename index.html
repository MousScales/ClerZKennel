Our Story

A Home, Not a Kennel

ClerZ Kennel is a family-operated boutique breeder on Connecticut's East Shoreline, close to the Rhode Island border and within reach of Massachusetts, New York, and New Hampshire. We raise healthy, well-socialized Toy Poodle and Shih Tzu mix puppies — each one inside our home alongside both parents.

Our puppies carry a unique Cafe Au Lait coloring and a hypoallergenic coat — hair, not fur, which means virtually no shedding. They are cared for every day, socialized with our family, and begin life with pee-pad training, crate training, and house-breaking before they ever leave us.

Every puppy receives a veterinary wellness check, age-appropriate shots, and deworming before going home. We are selective about placements — each puppy is matched carefully with a loving family where they will thrive. We never ship our puppies alone; all adoptions happen in person at our Connecticut home.
